# Highlights

## Make a highlight

Select text with the mouse. A popup appears next to the selection.

- Click a color swatch to highlight the passage in that color. Hover over a swatch, or focus it with the keyboard, to preview the color on your selection.
- Press a number key, 1 to 9, to pick the swatch in that position.
- Click **Note** to highlight in the color you used last and start writing about the passage in its card. See [Book notes](book-notes.md).
- Click the copy button to copy the passage text to the clipboard.

A selection that runs across a page break becomes one highlight per page. A keyboard selection (Shift with the arrow keys) shows the same popup at the end of the selection.

Press Escape or click elsewhere to close the popup. Closing it also clears the selection, and that click does nothing else, even over a highlight.

Highlights save as you make them. There is no save button.

## Change or delete a highlight

Click a highlight to open its popup.

- Click a swatch to change its color. The quote's color strip in the book note updates at once.
- Click the note button to write about the passage in its card.
- Click the copy button to copy the passage text.
- Click the delete button, or press Delete or Backspace, to remove the highlight. A notice offers **Undo**.

Hovering over a highlight darkens it. A small dot on a highlight means you've written something about it, in its quote note or under it in the book note.

## Highlight mode

In highlight mode, every passage you select with the mouse is highlighted right away, with no popup.

- Turn it on or off with the highlighter button in the Reader header, the **H** key, or the **Toggle highlight mode** command.
- Right-click the highlighter button to choose the mode's color. This also turns the mode on.
- While the mode is on, the button shows the chosen color. Reader remembers both the mode and the color.
- The color starts as Primary, and returns to Primary if you remove the chosen color from your palette.

Keyboard selections still show the popup.

## Commands for the current selection

Two commands act on whatever text is selected in a Reader tab, so you can give them hotkeys:

- **Highlight selected PDF passage** highlights in the last used color.
- **Highlight selected PDF passage and write about it in the book note** does the same as the **Note** button.

## Colors

You choose the palette in [Settings](settings.md#highlight-colors). Highlights remember a color by its ID, not its shade, so renaming or recoloring a palette color updates every highlight that uses it. A highlight whose color you removed turns gray until you give it another color.
